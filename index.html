About Me

I am a data scientist finishing an M.S. in Data Science at the University of Maryland, with nearly four years of professional experience turning operational data into decisions. My work sits at the point where machine learning meets something real: I build the models, but I also build the evaluation that tells you whether they actually worked.

Most recently that has meant NLP research: hand-labeling a corpus, training an extractor that beat the rule-based baseline four times over, and then measuring the human ceiling to show honestly how much headroom was left. It has also meant shipping production software through my consultancy: clinical documentation tools, revenue-cycle analytics, and LLM-backed scoring pipelines that paying users depend on.

My Projects

NLAH-Builder: NLP Research

Structured intent extraction from plain English. I led data and evaluation: 763 hand-labeled gold values, a trained extractor that lifted micro F1 from 0.05 to 0.20 under leave-one-out cross-validation, and a measured human ceiling of 0.30 that showed the labels were the real limit.

Clinical Notes Platform

AI-assisted clinical documentation for behavioral health providers. Flask and PostgreSQL, with a server-side LLM proxy so credentials never reach the browser, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and a 15-minute HIPAA session timeout.

Denial Report Builder

Upload a payer denial spreadsheet, get back a ranked report mapped to a denial category matrix so billing staff work the highest-recovery denials first. Auth, subscription billing, and Stripe webhooks included.

Ylunga Tenders

A bilingual procurement scout tracking six government sources across two countries, scoring every opportunity 0–10 with an LLM under separate French and English prompts, and drafting the response letters.

Evento: Event Ticketing

A full-stack ticketing platform built for the Cameroonian market, with Orange Money and MTN Mobile Money payments through CinetPay. React 19, Express, and PostgreSQL.

Algorithmic Trading Research

Systematic strategy research on equity and crypto markets: feature engineering from OHLCV data, gradient-boosted signal models, and a screener scoring volatility, beta, drawdown, and Sharpe against threshold rules.
